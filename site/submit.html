Good to know
  • You get your access code once the Finance team confirms your payment.
  • Submit one entry per competition. Each one gets a participant number by email.
  • Keep your name and school out of your files, file names included: judges only see your participant number.
  • Files up to 20 MB upload here. For bigger videos, share a Google Drive link set to “Anyone with the link”.
