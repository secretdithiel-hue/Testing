About us

Fifty years of keeping the Social Sciences in the conversation

Husay ng SokSay is organized by the UPLB Sociology Society, the first organization for Sociology majors in the University of the Philippines Los Baños.

Founded 1975

UPLB Sociology Society

The UPLB Sociology Society is a duly recognized academic organization in the University of the Philippines Los Baños and the first organization for Sociology majors in UPLB. Since its founding in 1975 it has stayed true to its mission: promoting the discipline of Sociology and the Social Sciences.

Now in its 50th year, the Society brings that mission beyond the campus through Husay ng SokSay, a competition that invites young people across the country to look at their own communities the way social scientists do.

Why Husay ng SokSay

The center of our society is the Social Sciences

The Social Sciences, or SokSay, help us read the pages of history still to be written. Yet despite their undeniable relevance, they still get little attention. Through the years, Husay ng SokSay has set out to introduce, remind and seal the relevance of the Social Sciences in our communities by organizing a series of competitions open to Junior High School and Senior High School students.

Every edition has a theme that asks students to examine the narratives around them critically: how biases can distort the truth, and how knowledge can be reshaped to reflect justice and equity.

What we stand for

Three things every entry is about

A critical lens

We ask students to question the stories society tells about itself, and to back their answers with the tools of the Social Sciences.

Lived stories

The best entries start from everyday Filipino realities: a family, a barangay, a classroom, a commute.

Fair play

Entries are judged anonymously under participant numbers, plagiarism disqualifies, and AI-made work is not allowed.

Our story

From 1975 to today

  1. 1975

    The Society is founded

    The UPLB Sociology Society becomes the first organization for Sociology majors in UP Los Baños, with a mission to promote Sociology and the Social Sciences.

  2. 2022

    1st Husay ng SokSay

    The first edition carries the theme “Panlipunang Pagkatuto, Hulmahan ng Diskurso” and reaches schools through a DepEd advisory.

  3. 2024

    2nd Husay ng SokSay

    The second edition includes a short film competition with a People's Choice Award.

  4. 2025

    3rd Husay ng SokSay

    “Muling Pagsusuri ng Kaalaman: Hamunin ang mga Naratibo, Hubugin ang Katotohanan.” Entries run from March 3 to 31, 2025, and winners are announced at a special webinar on April 11.

  5. 2025

    A golden year

    The UPLB Sociology Society celebrates 50 years since its founding in 1975.

  6. 2026

    4th Husay ng SokSay

    “Mga Kwentong Buhay: Hamunin ang mga Naratibo, Balangkasin ang Katotohanan.” Registration from January 17 to February 13, an orientation, a two-day workshop, and an awarding ceremony in Los Baños on March 21, 2026.

  7. 5th

    Next: the 5th Husay ng SokSay

    Being prepared now. Registration and entry submission will happen right here on this website. How does it work?

What participants get

More than a medal

  • Medals, plaques and cash prizes
    In the 4th edition: up to ₱2,000 for essay, speech and photography, and up to ₱5,000 for short film, plus special film awards.
  • Learning sessions
    The 4th edition opened with an orientation and a two-day workshop before entries were due.
  • A national stage
    Entries come from schools across the Philippines, from Pangasinan to Kidapawan.
