What is Husay ng SokSay?

Where high school students put the Social Sciences to work

The Social Sciences sit at the center of how we understand our society, yet they rarely get the spotlight. Husay ng SokSay, “excellence in the Social Sciences”, gives Junior and Senior High School students from all over the Philippines a stage to read their communities critically, challenge the narratives around them and tell the stories that matter.

Grades 7 to 12 Open nationwide Judged anonymously Join and submit online

How to join

From sign-up to spotlight in four steps

Everything happens on this website. No Google sign-in needed, and your answers go straight to the official registration list.

  1. 1

    Register

    Fill in the online form, pick your competitions and upload your filled-up registration form, school proof and a valid ID.

  2. 2

    Pay

    Pay the fee by GCash, Maya, BPI or PNB and upload a screenshot. Bundles and referral codes lower the price.

  3. 3

    Get confirmed

    Once your payment is checked you receive your Acknowledgement Receipt with your personal submission link and access code.

  4. 4

    Submit

    Upload your entry before the deadline. It is judged anonymously under a participant number.
