Republic Act 10173: Data Privacy Act of 2012

The UPLB Sociology Society collects the personal information in this form, such as your name, contact details, school, guardian and the documents you upload, only to run Husay ng SokSay: to check that you can join, to confirm your payment, to contact you and your guardian, to judge entries and to announce the results.

Only the organizing team can see your information. It is not shared outside the team except when the law requires it, and it is deleted once the competition no longer needs it. To see or correct your data, email husayngsoksayteam@gmail.com.

Ethical use pledge

The Husay ng SokSay team pledges to use your information and your entries only for this competition. Entries are judged anonymously. Your files are never sold or used for anything else, and they are only shown in public with your consent, for example a finalist's short film for the People's Choice Award.
