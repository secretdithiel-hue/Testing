FAQ

Questions, answered

Everything about joining, paying, submitting and judging. Can't find your answer? Message us.

JoiningWho can join?

Junior High School and Senior High School students (Grades 7 to 12) from any school in the Philippines.

JoiningCan I join more than one competition?

Yes, and bundles make it cheaper: 2 competitions cost ₱499 instead of ₱698, and all four cost ₱999. See Fees & bundles.

JoiningCan we join the short film competition as a group?

Yes. A team has up to 10 members, technical and production crew included. The team representative submits the film and lists every member in the entry form. Message us if you are not sure how your team should register.

RegistrationHow much is the registration fee?

₱349 for one competition, ₱499 for two, ₱749 for three. With the short film: ₱499 for the film alone, ₱649 with one other competition, ₱899 with two, and ₱999 for all four. A referral code takes ₱50 off, and some coupons give more discounts.

RegistrationWhat do I need to register?

Have these ready as photos or PDFs:

  • the filled-up Husay ng SokSay registration form (downloadable on the registration page)
  • proof of school affiliation, such as a school ID or certificate of enrollment
  • any valid ID
  • a screenshot of your payment
RegistrationHow do I pay?

By GCash, Maya, BPI or PNB. The account details appear on the registration form once registration opens. Pay the amount the form shows, take a screenshot, and upload it in the payment step.

RegistrationIs registering on the website the same as the Google Form?

Yes. Your answers go into the same official registration list, and you receive the same confirmation emails. Use one or the other, not both: an email address can only be registered once.

RegistrationDo I need a Google account?

Not on this website. You only need an email address you can open, because that is where your confirmation, receipt and access code arrive.

RegistrationWhat happens after I register?

You get a Registration Received email with your Gen ID right away. When the Finance team confirms your payment, you get your Acknowledgement Receipt: it contains your submission instructions and your personal access code for submitting on this website.

RegistrationWhat are referral codes?

Your Gen ID (it looks like REF-XXXXXXXX) is also your referral code. A friend who registers with it gets ₱50 off. When their payment is confirmed you earn cashback: ₱50 for your first paid referral and ₱10 for each one after that, released after the registration period.

RegistrationI made a mistake in my registration. What now?

Email husayngsoksayteam@gmail.com from the email address you registered with and tell us what to change. Please don't register twice.

SubmissionHow do I submit my entry?

Open the Open my submission page button in your Acknowledgement Receipt, or go to Submit entry and sign in with your email and access code. Pick the competition, fill in the details and upload your files.

SubmissionI lost my access code.

On the Submit entry page choose Email me my access code. It is sent to the email address you registered with.

SubmissionMy video file is too big to upload.

Files of up to 20 MB can be uploaded directly. For bigger videos, upload the file to your Google Drive, set General access to Anyone with the link, and paste the link in the form. We make our own copy, so keep the file until the results are out.

SubmissionCan I change my entry after submitting?

Each competition takes one entry per participant. If something went wrong, email the team before the deadline.

JudgingHow are entries judged?

Anonymously. After you submit, your entry gets a participant number and the judges only see that number, never your name or school. Keep your name, school, initials and signature out of your files, file names included.

JudgingCan I use AI tools?

No. The use of any artificial intelligence tool is strictly prohibited in all categories. An entry proven to have used AI is disqualified.

ResultsWhen are the winners announced?

At the awarding, which is announced on our Facebook page. The 4th edition's awarding was held on March 21, 2026 in Los Baños, Laguna.

PrivacyWhat happens to my personal data?

The UPLB Sociology Society collects your personal data only for this competition, in line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act 10173). Files you upload are kept in the organization's Google Drive and seen only by the organizing team; judges see anonymized copies.
