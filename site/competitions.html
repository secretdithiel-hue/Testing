The competitions

Four ways to tell a story

Open to Junior and Senior High School students (Grades 7 to 12) from any school in the Philippines. The mechanics below are from the 4th edition; the new event primer confirms each year's prompts and dates.

Essay writing · individual

Naratibo ng SokSay

Write a critical essay that answers the edition's guide question through the lens of the Social Sciences.

4th edition guide question
“How does the different perspective of Social Science challenge the existing realities faced by Filipinos and reveal the unrecognized truths in our society?”

Format

  • A4 size, Times New Roman, size 12, 1.5 spacing
  • Submitted as a PDF
  • English or Filipino
  • Leave out your name, school and any signature: entries are judged anonymously

How it is judged

  • Content50%Relevant, comprehensive, with an excellent application of knowledge in Social Science.
  • Organization30%Clear, well-expressed ideas with a smooth and creative flow.
  • Technicalities20%Correct grammar and spelling, and proper citation of sources.

Prizes (4th edition)

2₱1,500Medal + certificate
1₱2,000Medal + certificate
3₱1,000Medal + certificate
Speech · individual

Alingawngaw ng SokSay

Write an original speech on the theme and deliver it on video.

4th edition guide question
“How can the everyday experiences of Filipinos, combined with the practice and lens of Social Sciences, help in shaping our perspective of the lived realities of many Filipinos?”

Mechanics

  • Original title and original text, in Filipino or English
  • A video of 2 to 5 minutes, in landscape orientation; unedited videos are encouraged
  • Submit the written speech as a PDF together with the video
  • Plagiarism means disqualification, and awards given to plagiarized work can be revoked

How it is judged

  • Content50%Topic, ideas and message are relevant to the theme, well presented and creatively plotted.
  • Delivery30%Clear pronunciation, the right tone and fitting actions.
  • Technicalities20%Within the time limit, correct grammar, meets the technical requirements.

Prizes (4th edition)

2₱1,500Medal + certificate
1₱2,000Medal + certificate
3₱1,000Medal + certificate
Mobile phone photography · individual

Tatak SokSay: Imahe ng Lipunan

Take one photo with a mobile phone that reflects the theme, and explain the story behind it.

4th edition theme
“Capture an everyday occurrence of a Filipino life and showcase the deeper stories behind it that make up their realities through the lens of Social Sciences.”

Mechanics

  • Mobile phones or smartphones only; the photo must be your own and original
  • Cropping, brightness, contrast and similar adjustments are fine; removing things from the image is not
  • A title and a 150–200 word explanation in Filipino or English, laid out with the photo on an A4 page
  • One entry per participant; get permission from the people and property owners in your photo
  • No sensitive images, threats to national security or community peace, or discrimination against women and youth

How it is judged

  • Conveys the theme40%A relevant analysis of the community's social condition with a clear message.
  • Explanation30%Follows the word count and broadens the message of the image.
  • Perception of the photo20%Captures the attention of the audience.
  • Technicalities10%Original, taken only with a mobile phone, follows every rule.

Prizes (4th edition)

2₱1,500Medal + certificate
1₱2,000Medal + certificate
3₱1,000Medal + certificate
Short film · teams of up to 10

Salaysay, SokSay: Kwento ng Lipunan

Make a short film as a team. Finalists also compete for the People's Choice Award on Facebook.

4th edition theme
“Through the lens of truth and creativity, capture the untold stories of Filipino youth, challenge the narratives that define them, and inspire action to shape a brighter future.”

Guidelines

  • Up to 10 team members, technical and production crew included
  • 3 to 5 minutes long, not counting the closing credits
  • Royalty-free or original music only; copyrighted music means disqualification
  • No previously published or commissioned work, and no commercial brands or personalities
  • Finalists' films are posted on Facebook for People's Choice Award voting (with a signed consent form)

How it is judged

  • Originality25%
  • Cinematography and editing25%
  • Production and sound design20%
  • Creativity10%
  • Plot and characters10%
  • Call to action10%Does the film make viewers want to change something or spread the message?

Prizes (4th edition)

2₱3,000Plaque + certificate
1₱5,000Plaque + certificate
3₱1,500Plaque + certificate

People's Choice Award: plaque, certificate and ₱1,000. Special awards (medal and certificate): Best Actor, Best Actress, Best Director, Best Screenplay, Best in Film Score, Best in Production Design, Best in Cinematography & Editing.

Rules for every competition

Play fair, tell the truth

These apply to all categories. Breaking them disqualifies the entry.

  • No AI. Using any artificial intelligence tool is strictly prohibited in all categories.
  • Your own work. Plagiarized entries are removed, and awards can be revoked.
  • Stay anonymous. Keep names, schools and signatures out of your files; judges only see a participant number.
  • Real votes only. Bot accounts or auto-like schemes make an entry ineligible for the People's Choice Award.
